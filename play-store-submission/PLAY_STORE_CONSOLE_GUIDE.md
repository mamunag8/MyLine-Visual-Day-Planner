See your entire day clearly on a single proportional bar of time with MyLine: Visual Day Planner by kasb LLC — the 100% free, ad-free, local-first visual timeline planner, brain dump inbox, and anti-time-blindness focus timer.

Unlike traditional calendar and to-do apps that hide your schedule behind rigid grids or lock basic recurring tasks and routines behind $40–$80/year subscriptions, MyLine merges your daily events, flexible to-do tasks, and reusable routine stacks onto one calm, sensory-friendly visual timeline — 100% free forever, with zero accounts, zero servers, and zero ads.

✦ 1. PROPORTIONAL VISUAL TIMELINE (SEE YOUR FREE SPACE)
• True-to-Scale Time Blocks: A 15-minute task looks like 15 minutes, and a 2-hour deep work block looks like 2 hours so you never overbook your day.
• Automatic Free Space Pills: MyLine automatically detects gaps of 15+ minutes between your Wake-up and Wind-down anchors ("✨ 1h 15m Free Space — Tap to schedule") so you can fill open slots in one tap.
• Live Glowing "NOW" Needle: A warm terracotta horizontal needle moves down your timeline in real time, progressively shading your active task card so you can feel time passing at a glance.
• Native Calendar Anchor Overlay: Optionally overlay your phone's existing calendar meetings as locked anchor blocks (🔒) alongside your flexible tasks.

✦ 2. 1-TAP "REBUILD DAY" REFLOW ENGINE (NO MORE GUILT)
• Running Behind Schedule? Life happens. Whenever a flexible task runs past its scheduled time, tap "[ ✨ Rebuild Day ]" in the smart banner.
• Intelligent Reflow with Breathing Buffers: MyLine automatically rounds to the next 5-minute mark, flows your remaining flexible tasks around locked calendar appointments, and inserts a calm 5-minute breathing buffer between rescheduled blocks.

✦ 3. BRAIN DUMP INBOX & REUSABLE ROUTINE STACKS
• 2-Second Brain Dump Inbox: Capture random thoughts immediately without leaving your flow, and filter them by energy level (⚡ Quick 5m, 🔋 Low Energy, 🔥 High Focus).
• 1-Tap "Schedule Today": Drop any unscheduled inbox task straight into the next available Free Space gap on today's timeline.
• Reusable Routine Stacks: Start fast with pre-built multi-step templates like "Morning Launchpad (30m)" and "Evening Shutdown (25m)", or build your own custom routine stacks and drop them into Today in one tap.

✦ 4. ANTI-TIME-BLINDNESS VISUAL FOCUS TIMER & BROWN NOISE
• Counterclockwise Shrinking Pie Dial: Inspired by physical visual timers, watch a solid pastel wedge physically shrink every second alongside clear tabular countdown numbers.
• Active Subtask Spotlight: Displays only the single current micro-step with a "[ ✓ Done — Next Step ]" button so you never feel overwhelmed.
• "+5m Need More Time" Cascade: Extend your active focus block by 5 minutes and automatically cascade adjacent flexible tasks down.
• Built-In Offline Brown Noise: Toggle warm, procedural Brown Noise audio to quiet racing thoughts—100% offline with zero streaming data.

✦ 5. 100% LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY & ZERO-COST BACKUP
• Warm Tactile Paper & Midnight Slate Themes: Sensory-friendly Light and Dark palettes designed to reduce visual glare.
• Complete Data Ownership: All data is stored locally in on-device SQLite with Android Auto-Backup support plus 1-tap JSON Export & Restore to your own Google Drive or local storage.
