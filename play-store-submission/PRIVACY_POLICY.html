Privacy Policy — MyLine: Visual Day Planner

Effective Date: October 21, 2026
Developer: kasb LLC (com.kasbllc.myline)

1. 100% Local-First & Zero-Server Architecture

MyLine: Visual Day Planner is built with a strict 100% local-first, serverless architecture. We do not operate any backend servers that collect, store, track, or process your personal information, tasks, routines, or calendar events.

  • No Accounts Required: You never have to create an account or provide an email address or phone number.
  • Local SQLite Storage: All tasks, subtasks, routine stacks, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device.
  • Zero Advertisements & Zero Analytics SDKs: MyLine contains no third-party advertising networks or behavioral tracking SDKs.

2. Device Permissions

  • Calendar Access (READ_CALENDAR / WRITE_CALENDAR): Optional permission used solely on-device to display your existing phone calendar events as locked anchor blocks on your visual timeline so you can see your free space. Calendar data never leaves your device.
  • Local Notifications & Alarms (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / VIBRATE): Used strictly locally on your device to alert you when a focus timer or scheduled task starts or finishes.

3. Data Backup & User Ownership

Your data belongs 100% to you. MyLine supports standard Android OS Auto-Backup (android:allowBackup="true") to your personal Google account and provides a manual 1-tap JSON Export/Restore tool so you can save backups to your own local storage or personal cloud drive.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact kasb LLC via our developer support listing on Google Play.
